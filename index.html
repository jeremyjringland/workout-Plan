Runner Strength & Core Form Guide (35-40 Minutes Max)

Equipment needed: Kettlebell (KB), Dumbbells (DB), and Flat Bench. Rest 45-60 seconds between sets to maintain density.

Level 1: Anti-Extension Core & Glute Base

Focus: Preventing lower back hyperextension when fatigue hits late in long runs.

  • 1. Deadbugs (3 sets x 10 reps/side) Lie back flat, press lower back into floor. Extend opposite arm/leg under control without letting spine arch.
    Watch Demonstration Video
  • 2. Kettlebell Suitcase Carry (3 sets x 40 sec/side) Hold heavy KB in one hand at side. Walk tall without leaning sideways.
    Watch Demonstration Video
  • 3. Kettlebell Romanian Deadlift (3 sets x 10-12 reps) Hinge hips backward with flat spine. Squeeze glutes at top.
    Watch Demonstration Video
  • 4. Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat (3 sets x 8 reps/leg) Rear foot elevated on bench. Lower hips vertically to strengthen quads/glutes.
    Watch Demonstration Video

Level 2: Lateral Core & Pelvic Control

Focus: Oblique endurance and pelvic stabilization to eliminate torso sway.

  • 1. Side Plank Hold (3 sets x 30 sec/side) Elbow under shoulder, stack feet or place top foot on bench. Drive hips elevated.
    Watch Demonstration Video
  • 2. Bench Dumbbell Bird-Dog Row (3 sets x 8 reps/side) Knee and hand on bench, extend opposite leg straight back. Row dumbbell without hips tilting.
    Watch Demonstration Video
  • 3. Single-Leg Calf Raise on Bench Step (3 sets x 15 reps/leg) Ball of foot on bench ledge. Lower slow over 3 seconds to bulletproof calf/Achilles.
    Watch Demonstration Video
  • 4. Single-Leg Bench Hip Thrust (3 sets x 10 reps/leg) Upper back resting on bench edge. Drive through heel to lock out hips at top.
    Watch Demonstration Video

Level 3: Rotational Power & Upper Drive

Focus: Thoracic spine mobility and upper back posture for efficient arm swing.

  • 1. Kettlebell Plank Drag-Through (3 sets x 8 reps/side) High plank over weight. Reach under torso with far hand to pull weight across floor.
    Watch Demonstration Video
  • 2. Bench Hollow Body Leg Raises (3 sets x 12 reps) Lie on bench holding edge behind head. Lower legs under full control.
    Watch Demonstration Video
  • 3. Dumbbell Bench Chest Press or Row (3 sets x 10 reps) Strengthen upper torso and posture muscles to prevent rounding in miles 10-13.
    Watch Demonstration Video
  • 4. Kettlebell Halos & T-Spine Bench Stretch (3 sets x 10 reps / 45 sec) Rotate kettlebell smoothly around head, then stretch elbows over bench edge.
    Watch Demonstration Video
